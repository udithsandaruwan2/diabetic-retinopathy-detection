About

A screening assistant, built as one product.

Retina is a desk for reading diabetic retinopathy stage from a fundus photograph. It is not a clinic, and it is not a device that replaces an ophthalmologist. The grade is there so a person can decide who should be seen sooner.

The network is EfficientNetB0, trained in two phases with batch-norm kept frozen, then a CORAL head that predicts whether the stage sits above each step of the scale. The screen you open is that same model: preparation, prediction, attention map, and a trace of the real layers.

Who made it

Developed by Udith Sandaruwan. The public site is udithsandaruwan.com.

The photographs used to train and test the model are a public fundus collection. Nothing uploaded in the screen is stored after the reading returns.
